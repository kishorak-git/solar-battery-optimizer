# Solar Battery Optimizer

A Python-based algorithm for optimizing solar battery 
charge and discharge cycles in IoT energy systems.

## The Problem
Solar energy systems waste stored energy through inefficient 
charge/discharge timing — especially in low-sunlight 
conditions common in rural and semi-urban areas.

## What This Does
- Analyzes solar input patterns
- Optimizes battery charge/discharge cycles
- Reduces energy waste in off-grid solar setups
- Designed for integration with IoT monitoring systems

## Stack
Python · Data Analysis · Energy Systems Logic

## Background
Built as part of research into sustainable IoT 
infrastructure — connected to the IoT River Monitor 
and environmental sensing work at EmbedronIX.
